I’m a theoretical physicist at the California Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of fundamental physics and mathematical reasoning.

My academic journey began early: I entered college at eleven and earned my first doctorate at sixteen. Today, my work explores string theory, symmetry, and super-asymmetry—with a particular appreciation for precise definitions.
